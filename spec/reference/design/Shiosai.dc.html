Plate VI · Questions

Before you come to the pier.

Anything else, write to table@shiosai.jp or call +81 465 68 2914 after 13:00.

How do I know if the boat sailed?

The harbour strip at the top of this page updates at 05:30 every morning. If we close, we also call and email you by 09:00.

What does the tide menu cost?

¥16,500 per guest for nine courses. The sake pairing is ¥7,800 for six pours. Tax and service are included.

Can you work around allergies?

Tell us when you book. Shellfish and gluten allergies need 72 hours, because we change what we keep from the nets.

How do I get to Manazuru?

Ninety minutes from Tokyo on the JR Tokaido line, then an eight minute walk down to pier 2. We send a map with your table note.

Can I bring children?

Yes, from age eight at the 17:30 seating. We serve a shorter five course note for them at ¥6,600.

Can I visit the boat?

On Sundays two guests can join the crew at 05:10. Ask when you book. It depends on the weather and the captain.
